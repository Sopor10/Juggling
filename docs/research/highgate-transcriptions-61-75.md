## Transcription method and checks

- For **A sailor went to sea**, the diagram displays the eight actions and the boundary event. I copied the two crossing passes and hurried self throws with their repeated-hand positions, followed by three ordinary passes and a self. The cycle closes after eight actions.
- **Countdown** and **Geometric countdown** show only the first hand phase. Their notes explicitly say each sequence repeats starting with the other hand. I doubled their 15- and 9-event sequences to make 30- and 18-beat cycles.
- **Fizz buzz** uses the 12-event sequence and matching diagram as shown. Its note describes a separate crossing-pass variant that doubles the cycle; that optional variant is not conflated with the straight-pass source graph here.
- **Jim's fizz buzz (synchronous version)** gives both 12-action rows, marks the hurried throws, says Anne passes straight and Ben crosses, and says the diagram repeats with the other hand. I encoded each hurried action on the same hand as the preceding throw and the second half with hands inverted.

For every new record, each event has one outgoing and one incoming causal edge. The diagrammed next-event arrivals resolve to the expected actor and hand; all edges advance one event interval and cycle offsets agree with the repeating boundary. The 2017 diagrams and the matching 2014 diagrams/sequences were visually compared. The five new source patterns are imported in the central catalog. The editor validity test covers every available preset; the batch validator checks each source record and its import.
