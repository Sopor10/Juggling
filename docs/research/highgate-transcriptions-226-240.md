## Reviewed but not transcribed

These patterns are in the requested 2017 span, but their timing or throw semantics need a dedicated transcription pass before they can be represented faithfully. I did not infer edges from the sequence alone.

- **Four person mild madness feed** (`highgate-fourpersonmildmadnessfeed`) and **Four person Rachel’s feed** (`highgate-fourpersonrachelsfeed`) (p. 228): staggered half-beat starts; hold and zip notation; Rachel’s diagram shows only half the repeating hand phase.
- **Four person period-five feeds** (`highgate-fourpersonperiodfivefeeds`, section heading; pp. 229–233): `highgate-fourpersonparsnipfeedingfivecountpopcorn`, `highgate-fourpersonfunkybookendsfeedingfiveclubwhynot`, `highgate-fourpersonmartinsonecountfeedingwhynot`, `highgate-fourpersonmartinsonecountfeedingmaybe`, `highgate-fourpersononecountfeedingfiveclubwhynot`, and `highgate-fourpersononecountfeedingparsnip`. These use zip/heff throws and staggered starts; several also change club count or initial hand. The source says the list is non-exhaustive.
- **Box patterns** (pp. 235–238): `highgate-mildmadnessbox`, `highgate-whynotbox`, `highgate-maybebox`, and `highgate-972box` use zip/heff timing. The 972 box also has alternating double/single passes, nonuniform start phases, and initial-hand exceptions.

The “Line Feeds” section heading, `Two count line feed`, and `Urban terror feed (one count line feed)` are all on printed p. 240. Their inventory PDF-page field is inconsistent with the body page and footer, so this standalone source reference follows the PDF page image/text directly. No central catalog or inventory entries were changed.
